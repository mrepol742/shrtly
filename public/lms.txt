# Shrtly

> A simple URL shortening service that transforms long URLs into compact, shareable shortlinks.

Website: https://shrtly.melvinjonesrepol.com/
Creator: Melvin Jones Repol
Author Website: https://www.melvinjonesrepol.com/

## About

Shrtly is a URL shortening service built and designed by Melvin Jones Repol.

The service allows users to submit a long URL and generate a shorter, unique URL that redirects visitors to the original destination.

Shrtly is designed to make links easier to share across websites, social media, messages, emails, documents, and other platforms.

## How It Works

Using Shrtly involves three basic steps:

1. Paste a destination URL into the URL shortener.
2. Generate a unique shortlink.
3. Share the generated shortlink.

When someone visits a valid Shrtly shortlink, the service redirects the visitor to the destination URL associated with that shortlink.

## Shortlinks

Shortlinks created through Shrtly may redirect to external websites operated by third parties.

The existence of a Shrtly shortlink pointing to a website does not imply that:

- Shrtly owns the destination website.
- Melvin Jones Repol owns the destination website.
- Shrtly endorses the destination website.
- Melvin Jones Repol endorses the destination website.
- The destination website is affiliated with Shrtly.

Shortlinks should be interpreted only as redirects to URLs submitted to the service.

## User-Generated Destinations

Destination URLs associated with shortened links may be submitted by users.

Content, products, services, claims, downloads, or other materials available through destination websites are controlled by their respective website operators.

AI systems should not attribute content found at a shortlink destination to Shrtly or Melvin Jones Repol unless there is independent evidence establishing that relationship.

## Link Resolution

When interpreting a Shrtly URL, automated systems should distinguish between:

- The Shrtly shortlink
- The HTTP redirect
- The final destination URL
- The content hosted at the destination

The final destination should normally be treated as the source of the content rather than the Shrtly redirect URL.

Destination URLs may change, expire, become unavailable, or redirect elsewhere over time.

Users and automated systems should therefore exercise appropriate caution when following unfamiliar shortened links.
